Is my story really anonymous?+
Yes. You don't need to provide your real name or any identifying info. Just an email to receive your reply. Your story is never shared with anyone.
Who writes the replies?+
Every reply is written by a real person — with care, understanding, and sincerity. Not a template. Not AI-generated. Just human-to-human warmth.
Can I get a refund?+
If you're unsatisfied with the response, you can request a refund within 7 days. We hope every reply brings you a little warmth.
